workspace members

GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/members - get all members
GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/members/{userId} - get a member

POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/members - add a new member
PATCH /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/members/{userId} - update a member role (ONLY ROLE)
DELETE /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/members/{userId} - delete a member

POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/leave - deletes the current user as member
